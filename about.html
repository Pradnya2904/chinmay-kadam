ABOUT ME

Planning cities means understanding the people, land and systems behind them.

I’m Chinmay Kadam, an urban planning and public sector advisory professional with a background in civil engineering and town and country planning.

My work spans urban renewal, redevelopment, land-use planning, procurement, feasibility and public sector advisory, with professional project experience in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh.

Since 2019, I have contributed to multidisciplinary assignments that bring together planning, infrastructure, policy and implementation. I have worked with government departments, consultants, landowners and project teams on planning studies, survey coordination, redevelopment assessments, bid processes and decision-support materials.

Across assignments, I focus on turning complex project information into clear planning inputs and practical recommendations. My experience includes work related to Thane’s urban renewal programme and housing and infrastructure advisory in Madhya Pradesh.

Project experience described on this website was gained through professional employment. It does not imply endorsement by an employer or public authority.
