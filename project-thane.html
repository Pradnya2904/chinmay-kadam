Thane Urban Renewal Programme

Planning and implementation support for urban renewal schemes, including layout planning, survey coordination, entitlement analysis and feasibility inputs.

Thane, MaharashtraJuly 2019 — December 2024Thane Municipal Corporation

Overview

The urban renewal programme covered more than 300 hectares across multiple pockets of Thane city. The work brought together inclusive rehabilitation planning, development potential, land strategy and coordination across public and private stakeholders.

Assignment role

Civil cum Urban Planner, through employment with TECHCOMM Urban Management Consultants & Service Providers.

My contributions included urban renewal planning, layouts, FSI and rehabilitation calculations, survey coordination, land ownership assessment, eligibility SOP support, stakeholder coordination and feasibility inputs.

Selected contributions

  • Prepared and revised master zoning and detailed layout plans, including Kisan Nagar master planning covering 60 hectares.
  • Supported rehabilitation entitlement and FSI calculations, and drafted beneficiary and structure eligibility SOP inputs.
  • Coordinated geospatial and socio-economic survey work and analysed data relating to more than 29,000 dwelling units.
  • Contributed land ownership, valuation, compensation and land-swapping analysis.
  • Provided feasibility inputs for land parcels exceeding 30 hectares in collaboration with project partners.
  • Supported planning work considering UDCPR, CRZ, ESZ and Development Plan provisions.

Scope indicators

The programme scope exceeded 300 hectares. Survey-data analysis related to 29,000+ dwelling units, Kisan Nagar master planning covered 60 hectares, and separate detailed scheme references include a 6-hectare Kisan Nagar scheme. Feasibility work covered 30+ hectares. These are distinct scope references and should not be combined.
